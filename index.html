Good to know · FAQ

Before you take the next step

Clear, transparent answers about our planned services, migration advice scope, and launch timeline.

01 Is Hikoshi Global open for business?

Not yet. Hikoshi Global is preparing to launch in Dubai. Services, fees and availability will be confirmed before enquiries or payments are accepted.

02 Can you guarantee a job, visa or admission?

No. Employers, government authorities and education providers make those decisions. Assistance cannot guarantee an outcome.

03 Can I get Australia, Canada or New Zealand migration advice here?

Not through this preview. The Migration pages give general information only. Migration support for Australia, Canada and New Zealand is planned. Before launch, Hikoshi plans to engage registered or licensed professionals in each country, and will not give immigration advice or assistance without them.

04 Why does each country have its own migration page?

Each government sets its own visa types, forms and rules, and each regulates who may advise. The country pages keep those differences separate. They point to official sources instead of repeating rules that change.

05 Do you sell passports or citizenship?

No. No one can sell a passport. For citizenship- and residency-by-investment programs, each government sets the rules and makes every decision. Hikoshi is preparing an enquiry and application-support service only.

06 Which countries does worldwide job search cover?

Job-search support is planned worldwide, not only for Dubai, Australia or Canada. Tell us your target country. Work rights, employer requirements and sponsorship conditions differ by country, so the support we can provide will be checked before any paid service is agreed. Migration guidance is focused on Australia, Canada and New Zealand; the other planned services have their own scope.

07 Can I plan a holiday or a tourist visa for Dubai or another country?

That is part of the plan. You will be able to enquire about a custom trip for Dubai or another destination, including your preferred flights, stay and activities. No package, supplier, price or inclusion is confirmed in this preview. A written proposal and terms must be agreed before any booking or payment.

08 What will the services cost?

Pricing is not published yet. The scope and fee will be set out before any paid service is agreed.

09 Does the enquiry form send my details?

No. This is a website preview. The form only demonstrates the experience in your browser. Nothing is sent or saved, and no place on a waiting list is created.

Migration

Migrating to Canada

← All migration pages

Canada offers federal and provincial routes to permanent residence. This page lists the main ones to explore and where to confirm the rules.

General information onlyThis page is not immigration advice and Hikoshi does not offer migration services yet. Rules and fees change, so confirm each requirement with the official source below before you act.

Main pathways to explore

01Canada

Express Entry

An online system the government uses to manage skilled-worker applications. It covers three programs: Canadian Experience Class, Federal Skilled Worker and Federal Skilled Trades.

02Canada

Canadian Experience Class

For skilled workers who already have eligible Canadian work experience.

03Canada

Federal Skilled Worker and Federal Skilled Trades

For skilled workers with foreign or Canadian experience, and for people qualified in a skilled trade.

04Canada

Provincial Nominee Program

Provinces and territories nominate people whose skills and experience suit their economy and who want to live there. Some streams link to Express Entry.

05Canada

Work, study, family and visitor routes

Other Canadian routes sit outside Express Entry. We will map these with a regulated adviser once the service is ready.

What to start gathering

  • Passport and identity documents
  • Education records and, where needed, an education credential assessment
  • Work history with dates, duties and references
  • Language test results, if relevant to your pathway
  • Proof of funds or a job offer, if your pathway asks for it
Who can advise on CanadaThe Government of Canada says people who represent immigrants or give them advice should be a member in good standing of the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants, a law society (lawyers and notaries), or the Law Society of Ontario (paralegals, Ontario only). Hikoshi will not represent applicants or give Canadian immigration advice until a licensed representative is engaged.

Official sources

  • Express Entry (Government of Canada)
  • Express Entry: who can apply
  • Provincial Nominee Program
  • Check that your representative is authorized
  • Public register of consultants (CICC)

Migration

Migrating to New Zealand

← All migration pages

New Zealand uses invitation-based skilled residence alongside employer-linked work visas. This page lists the main routes to explore and where to confirm the rules.

General information onlyThis page is not immigration advice and Hikoshi does not offer migration services yet. Rules and fees change, so confirm each requirement with the official source below before you act.

Main pathways to explore

01New Zealand

Skilled Migrant Category resident visa

You submit an expression of interest first. You can apply only after Immigration New Zealand approves it and invites you to apply.

02New Zealand

Accredited Employer Work Visa

A work visa tied to an employer that holds accreditation. The employer and the job have to meet Immigration New Zealand's checks.

03New Zealand

Student and visitor visas

Study and short-stay routes are separate from work and residence. Conditions depend on the course or the visit.

04New Zealand

Partner, child and other visas

Family-based and other visa types exist. We will map them with a licensed adviser once the service is ready.

What to start gathering

  • Passport and identity documents
  • Education and qualification records
  • Work history with dates, duties and references
  • English test results, if relevant to your pathway
  • Health and character documents, which residence visas require
Who can advise on New ZealandNew Zealand's Immigration Advisers Authority says immigration advice, including advising on visa options or how to complete an application form, can only be given by a licensed immigration adviser or an exempt person. Hikoshi will not give New Zealand immigration advice until a licensed adviser is engaged. This page is general information only.

Official sources

  • Skilled Migrant Category resident visa (Immigration New Zealand)
  • Visa types (Immigration New Zealand)
  • Immigration Advisers Licensing Act 2007, section 6
  • Immigration Advisers Authority: who can give advice
