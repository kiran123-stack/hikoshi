Migration Pathway ★★★★

Migrating to Australia

Australia runs most skilled migration through an online expression of interest. This page lists the main pathways to explore and where to confirm the rules.

General information only

This page is not immigration advice and Hikkoshi does not offer migration services yet. Rules and fees change, so confirm each requirement with the official source below before you act.

Main Pathways ★★★★

Main pathways to explore

Key visa subclasses and skilled routes available for permanent and regional residence.

01 Australia

Skilled Independent (subclass 189)

A points-tested skilled visa. You submit an expression of interest first and must be invited to apply.

02 Australia

Skilled Nominated (subclass 190)

A points-tested skilled visa that needs nomination by a state or territory, after an expression of interest and an invitation.

03 Australia

Skilled Work Regional (subclass 491)

A provisional, points-tested skilled visa for people willing to live and work in a designated regional area.

04 Australia

Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186)

For skilled workers nominated by an Australian employer for a permanent visa. Occupation and employer conditions apply.

05 Australia

Study, work, visitor and family visas

Other visa types exist outside the skilled list. We will map these with a registered adviser once the service is ready.

What to start gathering

  • Passport and identity documents
  • Education and qualification records
  • Work history with dates, duties and references
  • English test results, if relevant to your pathway
  • Any skills assessment your occupation may need
Who can advise on AustraliaThe Australian Government says that anyone you appoint to give you immigration assistance, such as preparing a visa application, must be a registered migration agent, a legal practitioner or an exempt person. Hikkoshi prepares Australian visa applications and provides immigration assistance exclusively in partnership with registered migration agents (OMARA).

Official sources

  • SkillSelect overview (Department of Home Affairs)
  • Expression of interest (Home Affairs)
  • Types of skilled visas (Smart Move Australia)
  • Who can help with your application (Home Affairs)
  • Registered migration agents (OMARA)

Migration Pathway ★★★★

Migrating to Canada

Canada offers federal and provincial routes to permanent residence. This page lists the main ones to explore and where to confirm the rules.

General information only

This page is not immigration advice and Hikkoshi does not offer migration services yet. Rules and fees change, so confirm each requirement with the official source below before you act.

Main Pathways ★★★★

Main pathways to explore

Key federal programs, provincial streams and qualified employment routes.

01 Canada

Express Entry

An online system the government uses to manage skilled-worker applications. It covers three programs: Canadian Experience Class, Federal Skilled Worker and Federal Skilled Trades.

02 Canada

Canadian Experience Class

For skilled workers who already have eligible Canadian work experience.

03 Canada

Federal Skilled Worker and Federal Skilled Trades

For skilled workers with foreign or Canadian experience, and for people qualified in a skilled trade.

04 Canada

Provincial Nominee Program

Provinces and territories nominate people whose skills and experience suit their economy and who want to live there. Some streams link to Express Entry.

05 Canada

Work, study, family and visitor routes

Other Canadian routes sit outside Express Entry. We will map these with a regulated adviser once the service is ready.

What to start gathering

  • Passport and identity documents
  • Education records and, where needed, an education credential assessment
  • Work history with dates, duties and references
  • Language test results, if relevant to your pathway
  • Proof of funds or a job offer, if your pathway asks for it
Who can advise on CanadaThe Government of Canada says people who represent immigrants or give them advice should be a member in good standing of the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants, a law society (lawyers and notaries), or the Law Society of Ontario (paralegals, Ontario only). Hikkoshi coordinates Canadian immigration guidance and representation exclusively with licensed representatives (CICC or provincial law societies).

Official sources

  • Express Entry (Government of Canada)
  • Express Entry: who can apply
  • Provincial Nominee Program
  • Check that your representative is authorized
  • Public register of consultants (CICC)

Migration Pathway ★★★★

Migrating to New Zealand

New Zealand uses invitation-based skilled residence alongside employer-linked work visas. This page lists the main routes to explore and where to confirm the rules.

General information only

This page is not immigration advice and Hikkoshi does not offer migration services yet. Rules and fees change, so confirm each requirement with the official source below before you act.

Main Pathways ★★★★

Main pathways to explore

Skilled migrant categories, employer-linked accreditations and temporary permits.

01 New Zealand

Skilled Migrant Category resident visa

You submit an expression of interest first. You can apply only after Immigration New Zealand approves it and invites you to apply.

02 New Zealand

Accredited Employer Work Visa

A work visa tied to an employer that holds accreditation. The employer and the job have to meet Immigration New Zealand's checks.

03 New Zealand

Student and visitor visas

Study and short-stay routes are separate from work and residence. Conditions depend on the course or the visit.

04 New Zealand

Partner, child and other visas

Family-based and other visa types exist. We will map them with a licensed adviser once the service is ready.

What to start gathering

  • Passport and identity documents
  • Education and qualification records
  • Work history with dates, duties and references
  • English test results, if relevant to your pathway
  • Health and character documents, which residence visas require
Who can advise on New ZealandNew Zealand's Immigration Advisers Authority says immigration advice, including advising on visa options or how to complete an application form, can only be given by a licensed immigration adviser or an exempt person. Hikkoshi provides New Zealand immigration guidance in coordination with licensed immigration advisers (IAA). This page is general information only.

Official sources

  • Skilled Migrant Category resident visa (Immigration New Zealand)
  • Visa types (Immigration New Zealand)
  • Immigration Advisers Licensing Act 2007, section 6
  • Immigration Advisers Authority: who can give advice
